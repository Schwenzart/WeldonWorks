About Us

At Weldon Works we believe Landscaping & Garden Maintenance are built on recommendation and reputation, so we cover all aspects of Hard & Soft Landscaping.


Gardens that we design are always bespoke and we like to work in collaboration with our clients to achieve the highest standard regardless of the size or budget of a project.


With a range of suitable services undertaken by dedicated staff, we are confident that Weldon Works Landscaping will satisfy all your needs.


No matter what size your garden is, our expert landscapers provide excellent garden landscape services in Barnsley and the surrounding areas.


Based in Worsbrough, our landscaping team are here to help so contact us today .

Hard & Soft Landscaping


At Weldon Works we create beautiful and inspiring landscapes throught Barnsley to live, work and play in.


We take huge pride in our exceptional attention to detail, expert plant knowledge, creative solutions and award winning standards of workmanship.


The design and construction of garden features is a specialty of Weldon Works; From decking in hardwood or softwood and fencing to water features and ponds.
